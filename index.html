01 / A little about me

Curiosity, meet code.

I'm a Computer Science student at the American University in Cairo and a Software Engineering Intern at Microsoft. I work primarily with C++, Java, Swift, and Python, with a strong foundation in object-oriented programming, data structures, and algorithms.

I enjoy turning problems I've experienced into better tools. That led me to build acrars, a fork of the RARS RISC-V simulator focused on native desktop integration, keyboard navigation, and accessibility. Alongside building software, I've helped students work through core programming concepts as a teaching assistant.

acrars_

A more accessible, desktop-ready RISC-V simulator. A fork of RARS, rebuilt around the everyday experience of students using it.

  • Self-contained installers for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
  • Native file pickers, desktop menus, and keyboard navigation.
  • Screen-reader support and automated multi-OS releases.
